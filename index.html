How this room works

  • Exactly two people can ever register — after you, the door locks for everyone else.
  • Your passphrase never leaves your device in plain form; the room stores only a salted echo of it.
  • Messages, little reactions and “online” dots travel through your own private Worker — no third chat apps.
  • Everything your browser remembers (your token) lives in this browser only. Leaving wipes it.
